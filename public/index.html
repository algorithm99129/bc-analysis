Strategy generator

Each plan is a looping rule for one target: wait until its Since reaches a share of the adjusted gap (or start right away), bet until the first hit or a set number of bets (every-round plans bet each round of the cycle), then wait for the trigger again — until you stop it. Staking is flat, step-up (double after ⅔ of the bets), recovery (each stake sized to win back the cycle's losses) or every-round, and no cycle stakes more than your budget. Plans are rebuilt after every round from the history up to that round: each is simulated over the next period from the default chance, and history replays the same rule over every past period. Risk = 50 × chance of ending down + 50 × the 1-in-20 bad-case loss as a share of the budget. Every bet returns 99% of its stake on average, so every plan's expected total multiplier is ×0.990 — plans differ in risk and spread, not in expected value. For reference only; you place any bets yourself.

Range density

Target Hits / exp. Density Expected Diff Ratio Deviation Density vs expected

Density = hits ÷ rounds × 100. In a short range large differences are normal; the Deviation column shows whether a difference is unusual for the range size.

Deviation

Deviation = (hits − expected) ÷ standard deviation. Above 0: the target has hit more often than its default chance; below 0: less often. Shaded bands mark ±1σ and ±2σ — inside ±2σ is ordinary variation.

Probability board

Target Default Avg every Hits / exp. Observed Deviation Adj. gap Since Due Next in Status Longest Alert

Default = 99% ÷ target (1.98% for 1.00×), average gap = 1 ÷ default. Adj. gap scales the average gap by observed ÷ default rate; Due grows each round since the last hit. Note: the game's own chance for any single round stays at the Default value.
